## What these numbers do and do not prove

### They support

- substantial test/validation activity
- explicit regression-minded development
- a non-trivial system/interface surface
- deliberate separation between prototype evidence and live-runtime claims

### They do not prove

- production readiness
- final target-PC deployment
- complete hardware validation
- live model-provider availability
- production-scale reliability
- all 302 inventoried tests passing in the later reconciliation
